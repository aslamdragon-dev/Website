The estate

A resort that you own a piece of.

Artura sits on rising ground on the sheltered north-west coast, between an established village and the sea. The lagoon is shallow and calm here, the reef sits a few hundred metres out, and the beach at the foot of the estate is public but rarely busy.

Rather than sell finished apartments, Artura sells the land and keeps the resort. Buyers take a freehold plot and build the house they want; the clubhouse, the grounds and the concierge team are run for the estate as a whole.

Location
